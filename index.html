Story Behind

PARVAII?

Every Silhouette, a Celebration of Grace & Honest Comfort.

Clothes have always been so much more than mere fabric and seams to me. Ever since I first draped an unbleached handloom Chanderi kurti, I fell in love with that quiet, effortless feeling when an outfit simply feels right.

“

Maybe it’s the whisper of pure mulberry silk against the skin, the way the drape falls with every step, or the effortless confidence when you look in the mirror and smile with genuine delight.

Through years of exploring traditional Indian looms, styling everyday ensembles, and connecting with women who desire authentic luxury without stiffness, one thought kept returning: What if we could create pieces that feel deeply special yet remarkably effortless to live in?

My choices, my love for honest silhouettes, the confidence, breathability, and that unmistakable joy when a kurti flatters without demanding effort.

And that is how Parvaii was born—named after the classical word for "A Perspective of Grace".

Parvaii is simply our heartfelt collection, woven slowly by master multigenerational artisans in nocturnal midnight navy (#0B162A) and raw champagne sand (#C9B7A5) silks. Shared with you in the hope that whenever you slip into one, you feel grounded, radiant, and completely at home in your own elegance.

The Parvaii Atelier Founder & Creative Director
